        • Which oil: straight (non-dispersant) mineral oil for the first 50 hours of a new or overhauled engine, or until oil consumption stabilizes, and again after a cylinder change until consumption stabilizes. Ashless dispersant oil after that.
          • 
          
        • Switching after long use on mineral oil: after the first oil change to ashless dispersant, change again within 5 hours. If sludge shows up, keep changing every 10 hours until filters and screens come out clean, then go back to normal intervals.
          • 
          
        • Cold starts: below 10°F (−12°C), preheat the engine and oil (Lycoming SI 1505).
          • 
          
        • Additive: the LW-16702 additive requirement in this SI is for O-320-H and O/LO-360-E engines. It doesn't list the O-360-A1A.
          • 
          
        • Brands: any brand that meets these SAE specs is acceptable. They replace the former MIL-L-6082 (mineral) and MIL-L-22851 (ashless dispersant).
          • 
        
        
        
        Lycoming Service Instruction 1014N, Lubricating Oil Recommendations, 29 July 2020 (Table 1 and Part II). The temperatures are a guide. Original IO-360-D chart (SM 2-5): MIL-L-22851 Type II (SAE 50) above 30°F, Type III (SAE 30 or 10W-30) below 30°F.
