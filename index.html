        Weather comes from the National Weather Service API (api.weather.gov): past and current times use the airport's METAR; future times use the airport's TAF for wind (TEMPO and PROB winds count toward the crosswind check), the NWS hourly forecast for temperature (US TAFs don't include it), and the latest METAR altimeter. Airports without a TAF, or times past the end of the TAF, use the NWS hourly forecast wind and say so. The FAA's aviationweather.gov doesn't allow apps on other sites to read it, so use the links above to cross-check. Airports, field elevations and runways come from OurAirports (public domain) and work offline; verify against the Chart Supplement. Weather needs a connection.
        
    
        Takeoff: Maximum Performance Take-Off Data, figure 5-3, page 5-3 (sod runway, flaps 20°, 1900–2500 lb, sea level to 7500 ft, 0–20 kt headwind; increase distance 10% for each 25°F above standard). Landing: Maximum Performance Landing Data, figure 5-5, page 5-9 (sod runway, no wind, flaps 40°, power off, 2500 lb only; reduce distance 10% for each 4 kt headwind). Crosswind: 15 kt demonstrated, page 2-15. Takeoff distances add the engine margin because the POH figures are for the original 210 hp IO-360-D; N411TA has the 180 hp O-360-A1A. Landing uses straight POH numbers. Results are planning figures for a new airplane flown by the POH technique.
